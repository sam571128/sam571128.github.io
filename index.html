About

I'm Cheng-Yuan Lee (Sam), an ML Infrastructure Engineer at Cognition, where I work on the systems that power large-scale model training.

My journey started in competitive programming — reaching purple on Codeforces and competing extensively on AtCoder. That obsession with algorithmic problem-solving naturally led me into the world of ML systems, where efficiency and correctness are everything.

Previously, I've contributed to projects like Taiwan-LLM and worked on distributed training infrastructure. I studied at the University of Maryland, where I also taught competitive programming courses.
