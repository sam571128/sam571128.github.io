ABOUT ME

I’m Cheng-Yuan Lee (Sam), an ML Infrastructure Engineer at Cognition, where I build the systems that power large-scale model training.

My journey started in competitive programming — reaching purple on Codeforces and competing on AtCoder. That obsession with algorithmic problem-solving led me into ML systems, where efficiency and correctness are everything.

Previously, I contributed to Taiwan-LLM and worked on distributed training infrastructure. I studied at UMD, where I also taught competitive programming.
